# Static Fire Video Logs & Post-Inspection

> Visuals, high-framerate test footage, and qualitative post-burn hardware analysis.

## Overview
This directory contains the visual records of the solid rocket motor static test fires. While the DAQ system logs the quantitative thrust data, these video logs and post-inspection images provide the critical qualitative data required to assess plume dynamics, ignition transients, and hardware survivability.

## 1. Static Fire Footage
Video records of the F-class motor ignition, steady-state burn, and burnout phases. 
* **Plume Analysis:** Allows for visual verification of flow expansion, Mach diamond formation (if applicable), and exhaust stability.
* **Transient Events:** High-framerate playback is used to detect anomalies such as delayed ignition, chuffing, or asymmetric burn profiles that may not be immediately obvious in the raw load cell data.

## 2. Post-Burn Hardware Inspection
Photographic and video documentation of the motor and test stand immediately following the test and after motor teardown.

**Key Inspection Points:**
* **Thermal Liner Ablation:** Verifying the sacrificial liner successfully insulated the 25 mm outer casing from critical thermal degradation.
* **Nozzle Throat Erosion:** Inspecting the converging-diverging nozzle for asymmetric wear or throat widening, which directly impacts the $c^*$ efficiency and chamber pressure.
* **Stand Integrity:** Visual confirmation wether the stand was able to be stable.
* **Inspection Images:** High-resolution `.jpg` / `.png` capturing internal casing conditions and slag accumulation.
