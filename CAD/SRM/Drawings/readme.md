# Engineering & Manufacturing Drawings

> Formal 2D technical drafts and manufacturing specifications for the solid rocket motor and static test stand hardware.

## Overview
This directory contains the production-ready engineering drawings for all custom hardware. These documents establish the precise dimensions, tolerances, and material specifications required to transition the digital CAD models into physical, test-ready components.

### Motor Hardware
Detailed drafts for machining the primary pressure vessel and structural closures.
* **Motor Casing:** Specifications for the 25 mm outer diameter structural tube, including O-ring groove tolerances, wall thickness limits, and closure threading.
* **Closures & Nozzle:** Converging-diverging nozzle geometry and internal profiles designed to withstand peak chamber pressures and thermal flow.

## Standards & Formatting
All documents are provided in `.pdf` format for universal viewing by machinists or reviewers without requiring specialized CAD software. Drawings utilize standard Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) practices to clearly communicate functional limits and inspection criteria.
