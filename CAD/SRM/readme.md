# Solid Rocket Motor (SRM) & Propellant Mold

> Mechanical design files, manufacturing tooling, and engineering drawings for an F-class Solid Rocket Motor.

## Overview
This directory contains the core 3D CAD assemblies and formal 2D drafts for the solid rocket motor. It encompasses the structural motor casing, the nozzle geometry, and the custom 3D-printed tooling required to cast the solid propellant grains.

## Core Models

### 1. SRM Hardware Assembly
The primary rocket motor structure, modeled around a **25 mm casing diameter**. The assembly details the integration of the motor casing, forward/aft closures, the converging-diverging nozzle, and the internal sacrificial thermal liners.

### 2. Propellant Casting Mold
Custom tooling designed for casting the solid propellant into a **single-grain Bates configuration**. The mold is desgined to be placed into the rocket casing as a type of experimental thermal liner made of polycarbonate.

### 3. Engineering Drawings
Formal 2D manufacturing drafts (`.pdf`). These drawings detail the critical dimensions, tolerances, and GD&T required to machine the motor hardware and verify the 3D-printed mold tolerances.
